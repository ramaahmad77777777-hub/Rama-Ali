Cybersecurity · Post 01

Why cybersecurity matters to me

I chose to study cybersecurity because digital systems are part of almost everything we do. Every time someone opens an account, sends a message, or connects to a network, there is a need to protect information. I find it interesting that security depends not only on technical tools but also on the choices people make every day.

One idea I want to explore further is how small habits can reduce risk. Strong and unique passwords, careful handling of suspicious links, and regular software updates are simple examples. I also want to understand how networks work and how security professionals recognise unusual activity. Studying these topics encourages me to think carefully, ask questions, and look for evidence before making decisions. My goal is to build a foundation in cybersecurity step by step.

Key takeaway: Good security starts with informed everyday choices.

Drawing · Post 02

Drawing as a creative break

Drawing is one of my favourite interests because it gives me a different way to express ideas. A blank page can become a simple shape, a pattern, or a complete design. I like the idea that a drawing does not have to be perfect to be meaningful. The process of noticing small details is just as important as the final result.

When I think about improving my drawing, I focus on basic shapes, proportions, shading, and patience. Starting with light lines makes it easier to change a sketch before adding more detail. Drawing also reminds me that progress takes practice, which is a lesson I can apply to my studies. I hope to keep exploring different styles and find new ways to turn my ideas into images.

Reading · Post 03

What I enjoy about reading

Reading is another interest that I value because it introduces me to ideas beyond my immediate surroundings. A book or an article can explain a new topic, show a different point of view, or make me curious about something I have never considered before. I enjoy the idea of learning at my own pace and returning to a passage whenever I need more time to understand it.

I am interested in reading both for enjoyment and for learning. Technical reading can help me explore cybersecurity concepts, while other kinds of writing can build imagination and broaden my perspective. I think that a useful reading habit is to pause and reflect on what the author is trying to say. Over time, reading can strengthen my vocabulary, concentration, and ability to communicate.
